# Circuit references

Apex is an original circuit. The design takes driving problems and venue details from several countries. Its centreline, grades and banking are authored for SlopDrift.

| Reference | Track characteristic | Application at Apex |
|---|---|---|
| [Spa-Francorchamps, Belgium](https://www.spa-francorchamps.be/en/the-circuit) | Forest setting and a compression leading into a climb | Summit Ascension combines a low approach with a rising sequence and a late horizon reveal. |
| [Mount Panorama, Australia](https://www.bathurst.nsw.gov.au/Services/Facilities/Mount-Panorama/About-the-Mount/The-Circuit) | The Skyline crest changes the driver's view before the descending Esses. | Skyline Ridge delays the sight of the downhill road, with barriers and the outer landscape supplying orientation. |
| [Nürburgring, Germany](https://nuerburgring.de/news/nuerburgring-und-mercedes-amg-verlaengern-partnerschaft-bis-2026?locale=de) | The Nordschleife's concrete banked Karussell and the adjoining GP venue have distinct identities. | The Carousel changes both crossfall and surface. The pit straight carries the modern venue architecture. |
| [Suzuka, Japan](https://global.honda/en/F1/circuit/suzuka/introduction/) | Rhythm through successive esses and a figure-eight layout | The Serpent needs a linked approach. Separate physical decks allow the player to drive across and underneath the same junction. |
| [Daytona, USA](https://www.daytonainternationalspeedway.com/secure-your-experience/) | High banking faces the spectator stands. | Thunder Bowl eases into its 24° bank and brings the grandstand into view. Daytona's operator describes 31° banking at its high banks. |
| [Sebring, USA](https://www.imsa.com/sebring-international-raceway) | A former airfield gives the circuit its concrete surface character. | Heritage Sector changes surface material and wheel response, with visible slabs. |
| [Monza, Italy](https://www.monzanet.it/en/circuit/) | Long straights make braking references and corner entries important. | Velocity Straight has a long sightline and distance boards. |
| [Laguna Seca, USA](https://weathertechraceway.com/pages/track-information) | The Corkscrew combines direction change with an abrupt loss of elevation. | The Vortex links the crest, turn and downhill compression. |

Grandstand placement at the Red Bull Ring, public-road sections at Le Mans, and Paul Ricard's differentiated runoff also informed venue sightlines, the long-straight environment and edge treatments. [Red Bull Ring](https://www.redbullring.com/en/events-tickets/formula-1/formula-1-circuit/) · [Automobile Club de l'Ouest](https://www.24h-lemans.com/en/news/why-is-the-24-hours-of-le-mans-run-on-public-roads-58347) · [Circuit Paul Ricard](https://www.circuitpaulricard.com/en/the-circuit/technologies-innovations)

The research also covered the remaining circuits in the final brief. Silverstone and Barcelona informed the scale and circulation of a modern GP venue. Imola illustrates the relationship between a park, its public setting and a race circuit. Fuji offers a useful example of a long straight opening onto mountain scenery. Portimão and Interlagos show different ways that a crest or rolling grade can control sight distance, while Kyalami provides a comparison for a modernised facility with a distinct landscape. These are design inferences from the venue and series material. ## Race operations and simulator references

GT World Challenge Europe's series descriptions and sporting regulations informed session names, race services and endurance framing. The circuit operator's published Suzuka guide and FIA circuit safety resources informed the separation between pit operations, track and spectator areas. The park has no real-world licence or homologation. [SRO series overview](https://www.gt-world-challenge-europe.com/about/gt-world-challenge-europe) · [SRO regulations](https://www.gt-world-challenge-europe.com/about/regulations) · [Suzuka course guide](https://www.suzukacircuit.jp/course_s/pdf/suzuka_courseguide.pdf) · [FIA circuit safety](https://www.fia.com/circuit-safety)

Assetto Corsa Competizione informed the emphasis on GT race conditions. Gran Turismo 7 provided a reference for readable race presentation. Automobilista 2 provided a visual reference for weather variation, and Le Mans Ultimate provided a reference for the choices drivers make when configuring a race weekend.

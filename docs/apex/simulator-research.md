# GT simulation references and how they changed Apex

The final six configurations specified in the brief govern the circuit. Developer and publisher material was used to study observable behaviour. All new scenery, textures and entrant geometry are authored for SlopDrift.

| Simulator | What the developer documents | Application at Apex |
|---|---|---|
| Automobilista 2 | Reiza describes AI recognising usable lanes and taking different dry and wet lines. | Each opponent evaluates upcoming curvature, available width, nearby traffic and moisture. Drivers move away from deposited rubber in the wet. [Reiza's development update](https://forum.reizastudios.com/threads/automobilista-2-september-2026-development-update.36665/) |
| Gran Turismo 7 | The driving guide explains local yellow and lapping flags. The HUD manual shows sector comparisons against a session best. | Six flag states reach marshal panels. The HUD gives lap validity and sector deltas. Qualifying and time trials retain only valid times. [Flag guide](https://www.gran-turismo.com/us/gt7/apex/how_to_get_started/in_game/03) · [Race instruments](https://www.gran-turismo.com/us/gt7/manual/race/04) |
| Le Mans Ultimate | The race-weekend menu configures sessions, weather, regulations and time scale. | Practice leads to qualifying, the timing classification sets the grid, and race results can include a track-limits penalty. Lap count, opponent count, difficulty and weather are independent choices. [Race weekend guide](https://guide.lemansultimate.com/hc/en-gb/articles/13210950566927-Configuring-Race-Weekend) |
| Assetto Corsa Competizione | Kunos describes track rubber, marbles, moisture and time-dependent conditions. | Physical roads own a common rubber field for cars on all six layouts. Tyres gain dry grip and lose wet grip on the rubbered line. The asphalt shader reads the same field. [ACC](https://assettocorsa.gg/assetto-corsa-competizione/) |
| Assetto Corsa | Kunos documents laser-scanned track features and surface suspension feedback. | Kerbs, bank and changing road grades affect wheel contact in the normal vehicle simulation. Track construction begins with an original spline survey. [Assetto Corsa](https://assettocorsa.gg/assetto-corsa/) |
| iRacing | The rain guide describes differences in wet grip across the track and how drivers adapt to the changing surface. | Precipitation, spray visibility, surface roughness, lateral grip and AI pace share the session wetness state. [Rain Q&A](https://support.iracing.com/support/solutions/articles/31000172636-rain-q-a) |
| rFactor 2 | Studio 397's track technology guide explains the interaction of the driving surface and visible road detail. | Asphalt wear and runoff material use the same section data for rendering and tyre contacts. Overlapping deck selection accounts for the car's height. [Track technology guide](https://www.studio-397.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/rF2_Track_Technologyv3.pdf) |
| RaceRoom | The ADAC GT Masters manual describes weekend sessions, pit stops and race presentation. | Timed service choices and the player’s assigned box affect fuel, tyres and repair. AI use numbered stalls on layouts sharing the pit straight. [RaceRoom's GT Masters manual](https://www.raceroom.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/ADAC_GTM_Experience_2014_User_Manual_STEAM.pdf) |
| Project CARS 2 | Slightly Mad Studios describes rain gathering and drying differently across a circuit. | Standing-water shading varies across each road section. Moisture and friction change throughout a drying session. Water depth is a visual approximation. [Developer account on Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2017/09/22/project-cars-2-available-now-xbox-one/) |
| Forza Motorsport | Turn 10 describes time of day, track temperature, rubbering and their influence on racing. | Endurance lighting moves between daylight, sunset, night and dawn. A simplified thermal model makes the GT tyre grip respond to speed, slip, braking and conditions. [Turn 10's technical overview](https://forza.net/news/forza-motorsport-the-most-technically-advanced-racing-game-ever-made/) |

The circuit research covers the eight required track references and additional venues. [Real circuit design references](research.md)
